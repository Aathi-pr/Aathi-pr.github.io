# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Recruiters, hiring managers, and freelance clients who are evaluating Adithyan's ability to design and build thoughtful digital experiences. They are scanning quickly on a phone or laptop and need to understand the quality, range, and practical outcome of each project.

## Product Purpose

This portfolio presents Adithyan as a full-stack developer and designer through selected work, technical range, and clear ways to start a conversation. The project section should make strong work easy to scan first, then reward deeper exploration without hiding essential context.

## Brand Personality

Minimal, intentional, distinctly human. The experience should feel calm and assured, with enough interaction to make the work memorable without turning the portfolio into a demo of effects.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS card grids, loud gradients, oversized decorative UI, and interaction that makes project information difficult to access. Do not replace the current monochrome, typography-led identity with a colorful template aesthetic.

## Design Principles

- Lead with the work and let imagery carry proof.
- Make the first glance useful, then provide a deeper layer for curious visitors.
- Keep the visual system quiet so the projects and typography remain the focus.
- Use motion to reveal relationships and state, not to decorate every element.
- Preserve a clear path from project discovery to contact.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Aim for WCAG 2.2 AA contrast and keyboard access. Keep project information available without hover, support touch and small screens, respect reduced-motion preferences, and keep interactive targets comfortably tappable.
